      • 《百年孤独》

        *One Hundred Years of Solitude*

        作者: 加西亚·马尔克斯。

        内容: 描绘了布恩迪亚家族七代人的传奇故事，以及加勒比海沿岸小镇马孔多的百年兴衰，反映了拉丁美洲一个世纪以来风云变幻的历史。

        Author: Gabriel García Márquez.

        Content: It depicts the legendary stories of seven generations of the Buendía family and the century - long rise and fall of the small town of Macondo on the Caribbean coast, reflecting the changing history of Latin America over a century.

      • 《为权利而斗争》

        *The Struggle for Rights*

        作者: 鲁道夫·冯·耶林。

        内容: 本书以其犀利的语言和深刻的思想，阐述了权利的本质和为权利而斗争的重要性。

        Author: Rudolf von Jhering.

        Content: With its sharp language and profound thoughts, this book elaborates on the essence of rights and the importance of struggling for rights.

      • 《你的夏天还好吗》

        *Are You Okay This Summer?*

        作者: 金爱烂。

        内容: 书中收录的七个故事，展现了韩国女性在不同生活场景中的困境与挣扎。

        Author: Kim Ai - ran.

        Content: The seven stories in this book show the dilemmas and struggles of Korean women in different life scenarios.

      • 《当尼采哭泣》

        *When Nietzsche Wept*

        作者: 欧文·亚隆。

        内容: 以心理治疗为背景，讲述了尼采与布雷尔医生之间的心灵对话。

        Author: Irvin D. Yalom.

        Content: Set against the background of psychotherapy, it tells the spiritual dialogue between Nietzsche and Dr. Breuer.

      • 《鼠疫》

        *The Plague*

        作者: 阿尔贝·加缪。

        内容: 描写了北非一个城市在鼠疫肆虐时人们的生活状态和抗争精神。

        Author: Albert Camus.

        Content: It depicts the living conditions and fighting spirit of people in a North African city during the plague.

      • 《红玫瑰与白玫瑰》

        *Red Rose, White Rose*

        作者: 张爱玲。

        内容: 讲述了主人公振保与两位女性的情感纠葛，揭示了人性的复杂。

        Author: Eileen Chang.

        Content: It tells the emotional entanglement of the protagonist Zhenbao with two women, revealing the complexity of human nature.

      • 《人类简史：从动物到上帝》

        *Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind*

        作者: 尤瓦尔·赫拉利。

        内容: 从全新的视角讲述了人类从原始石器时代到 21 世纪的发展历程。

        Author: Yuval Noah Harari.

        Content: It tells the development process of human beings from the primitive Stone Age to the 21st century from a brand - new perspective.

      • 《历史深处的忧虑》

        *Concerns Deep in History*

        作者: 林达。

        内容: 通过讲述美国的一些社会现象和历史事件，探讨了美国的民主制度和文化。

        Author: Linda.

        Content: By telling some social phenomena and historical events in the United States, it explores the American democratic system and culture.

      • 《沧浪之水》

        *The Water of the Canglang River*

        作者: 阎真。

        内容: 以主人公池大为的成长经历为主线，反映了知识分子在现实社会中的无奈与挣扎。

        Author: Yan Zhen.

        Content: With the growth experience of the protagonist Chi Dawei as the main line, it reflects the helplessness and struggle of intellectuals in the real society.

      • 《与情绪和解》

        *Making Peace with Emotions*

        作者: 罗娜·雷纳。

        内容: 提供了应对情绪问题的方法和策略，帮助读者更好地管理情绪。

        Author: Ronna Rayner.

        Content: It provides methods and strategies for dealing with emotional problems to help readers better manage their emotions.
